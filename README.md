<h1 align="center">DevMarshal</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Your AI coding agents, marshalled into one team.</b></p>

DevMarshal runs the AI coding tools you already use — **Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, or a local model
through Ollama** — as one engineering team on your own projects. It plans the work, has it written, tested
and reviewed, saves each verified step in Git, and asks you only when a decision is yours, in plain language.
Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

**[⬇ Latest release](https://github.com/gr-buildstudio/devmarshal/releases/latest)**

| System | File | How |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `devmarshal-<version>-setup.exe` | Run it. No administrator rights needed. SmartScreen may warn (the installer is not signed): *More info* → *Run anyway*. |
| Linux (x64) | `devmarshal-<version>-linux-x64.tar.gz` | `tar -xzf devmarshal-*.tar.gz && cd devmarshal-* && ./install.sh` |

Check your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` in the same release.

**You need:** [Git](https://git-scm.com/downloads), and at least one AI coding tool installed and signed in on
your computer (Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI or Ollama). DevMarshal finds them on its first start. Your use of
those tools follows their own terms and quotas.

**Updates:** DevMarshal tells you when a newer version is published here (once a day; switch it off in
Settings). It never downloads or installs anything by itself.
